# Barrio — Attribution Notice

This repository contains a documented delivery by **ValenteCreativo**, recorded
in Git commit `3bf83f75c3186ce43f164e6fa3276573bada8701` on November 5, 2026.

The delivery covers the post-baseline product work included in that commit:

- the new BARRIO landing and its frontend implementation;
- the interactive map application for locating tianguis across CDMX, with
  search, market details, and filtering by operating day of the week;

- the deployable `public` application and Vercel configuration.
