# Barrio — Attribution Notice

This repository contains a documented delivery by **ValenteCreativo**, recorded
in Git commit `3bf83f75c3186ce43f164e6fa3276573bada8701` on November 5, 2026.

This delivery reflects the collaborative first product iteration by
**ValenteCreativo** and **[Arturo Vargas](https://github.com/ArturVargas)**
during [Chilangatón 2026](https://luma.com/j55anly0?tk=AR14er), a SEDECO
event. It covers the post-baseline product work included in that commit:

- the new BARRIO landing and its frontend implementation;
- the interactive map application for locating tianguis across CDMX, with
  search, market details, and filtering by operating day of the week;
- normalization and presentation of the local copy of the official **Ubicación
  de Tianguis** dataset from Datos Abiertos CDMX, including its geographic,
  location, schedule, alcaldía, and stall-count information;
- the editorial design system and graphic assets used in the experience,
  including illustrations, maps, mockups, and user-flow imagery; and
- the deployable `public` application and Vercel configuration.

It also records the WhatsApp-agent prototype led by Arturo Vargas to validate
inventory registration by merchants through WhatsApp, using machine learning
and vision-capable models to identify inventory from images. The planned API
flow to associate submitted images and inventory with a merchant's registered
tianguis was not completed in this delivery.

The project also incorporates a pre-existing baseline with earlier recorded
contributions by Ana1Pau and `Barrio Dev`, as shown in the Git history. Ana1Pau
and Fernando are also acknowledged for earlier shared ideation and parallel
exploration of a related social-product line for merchants and users.

Any future continuation should preserve this notice and provide appropriate
credit to ValenteCreativo, Arturo Vargas, and the recorded contributors. This
notice documents attribution; it does not by itself grant, waive, or transfer
any rights.
