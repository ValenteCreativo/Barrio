# Authors and contribution record

This file records authorship information preserved in this repository's Git
history. It is intended to keep attribution visible as the project evolves.

## ValenteCreativo

**GitHub:** [ValenteCreativo](https://github.com/ValenteCreativo)  
**Email:** valentecreativo@proton.me

ValenteCreativo authored and delivered the product iteration recorded in commit
[`3bf83f7`](https://github.com/ValenteCreativo/Barrio/commit/3bf83f75c3186ce43f164e6fa3276573bada8701), dated November 5, 2026. The delivery includes:

- a new BARRIO landing experience, including its information architecture,
  interaction design, frontend implementation, and responsive styling;
- the interactive `barrio-2` map application, including geographic rendering,
  market search, market-detail panels, and day-of-week controls;
- integration of the local copy of the official **Ubicación de Tianguis**
  dataset from Datos Abiertos CDMX: normalization of its geographic and
  descriptive fields, and filtering/presentation of tianguis by operating day,
  alcaldía, location, hours, and number of stalls;
- the editorial visual system and graphic assets used by the product, including
  the logo, original BARRIO illustrations, maps, product mockups, user flows,
  and supporting images; and
- the deployable `public` application and its Vercel deployment configuration.
